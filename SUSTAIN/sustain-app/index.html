How It Works

Three Simple Steps to Save Lives

From leftover feast to a life-saving meal — in under 60 minutes.

📋
1

List Your Surplus

Event organizer posts surplus food details — type, quantity, pickup time, and location.

🔔
2

NGOs Get Notified

Nearby registered NGOs receive instant alerts and can claim the food donation in one tap.

💝
3

Food Reaches the Needy

NGO picks up and distributes the food to shelters, streets, and communities in need.
